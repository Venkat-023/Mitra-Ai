# RVITM Institutional Overview, RSST Heritage, Vision, Mission & Governance

Affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi | Approved by AICTE, New Delhi
Managed by the Rashtreeya Sikshana Samithi Trust (RSST) / RV Educational Institutions (RVEI)

## 1. About RVITM & RSST Legacy
- **Establishment:** RVITM was established in **2019** as a premier technical institution under the aegis of the renowned **Rashtreeya Sikshana Samithi Trust (RSST)**.
- **Trust Heritage:** Founded in 1940 by the distinguished freedom fighter, social reformer, and visionary educationist **Sri M.C. Sivananda Sarma**, RSST has grown into one of India's most prestigious educational consortiums, steering over 25+ institutions encompassing basic education to advanced engineering, management, and health sciences.
- **Campus Setting:** Situated in Chaithanya Layout, 8th Phase, JP Nagar, Bangalore, the state-of-the-art urban campus is architecturally modern, fully ICT-enabled, and designed to foster experiential technical education.

## 2. Institutional Vision & Mission
- **Vision Statement:**
  > "To impart top-quality technical education with global competence and societal commitment, leading to breakthrough innovations."
- **Mission Statement:**
  > "Our mission is to nurture passionate engineers through experiential learning by providing state-of-the-art infrastructure and ICT-enabled education."
- **Core Values:** Academic excellence, integrity, societal responsibility, industry collaboration, and human-centric engineering.
