# RVITM Student Life, Technical & Cultural Clubs, Statutory Committees & Welfare Cells

## 1. Student Clubs & Co-Curricular Innovation
RVITM promotes a vibrant student ecosystem with active student-led chapters across technical, entrepreneurial, cultural, and community sectors:

### Technical & Engineering Clubs:
- **Phoenix Robotics Club:** Focuses on autonomous robotics, drone prototyping, embedded systems, and participating in national robotics competitions.
- **ECX (ECE Club):** Departmental electronics community conducting workshops on IoT, PCB design, microcontroller architectures, and technical hackathons.
- **Coding Club:** Fosters algorithmic thinking, competitive coding on platforms like LeetCode and Codeforces, open-source development, and web app hackathons.
- **E-Cell (Entrepreneurship Cell):** Nurtures student startups, incubation proposals, angel investor networking, and business plan bootcamps.
- **Science Club:** Interdisciplinary forum exploring fundamental physics, chemistry, and emerging green technologies.

### Cultural, Creative & Literary Clubs:
- **Cultural Club:** Organizes university fests, music ensembles, theatrical productions, and dance troupes.
- **Rudra:** The dedicated performing arts and music society of RVITM.
- **Kannada Sangha:** Celebrates Karnataka heritage, Kannada Rajyotsava cultural summits, poetry symposia, and regional literature.
- **Magazine & Editorial Club:** Publishes the annual college magazine and chronicles campus creative achievements.
- **Adventure Activities Club & Aegis:** Organizes trekking expeditions, environmental clean-up drives, cycling rallies, and leadership retreats.

## 2. Anti-Ragging Committee (ARC) & Squad
- **Zero-Tolerance Mandate:** In compliance with Supreme Court of India directives and AICTE / UGC Regulations, ragging in any form is strictly banned inside campus, hostels, college buses, and off-campus student hubs.
- **Anti-Ragging Committee (ARC):** Headed by the Principal (Chairman) and Vice-Principal, with senior professors, local police administration liaisons, and student representatives.
- **Anti-Ragging Squad:** Conducting surprise inspections across hostel corridors, dining areas, canteens, and sports fields.
- **National Anti-Ragging Helpline:** 1800-180-5522 (24x7 Toll-Free) | College Emergency Desk: 080-35095100.
- **Disciplinary Penalties:** Immediate suspension, rustication from institution, debarment from exams, and formal FIR filing under the Karnataka Prohibition of Ragging Act.

## 3. Institutional Governance & Disciplinary Committees
- **Disciplinary Action Committee (DAC):** Inquires into code of conduct breaches, campus vandalism, or procedural violations, ensuring fair student hearings.
- **Malpractice Cases Consideration Committee (MPCC):** Evaluates academic irregularities and exam conduct violations in accordance with VTU examination statutes.
- **Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) / Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH):** Dedicated confidential cell ensuring gender safety, equal opportunity, and immediate redressal of grievances for female students and staff.
- **Online Grievance Redressal Committee:** Transparent web-based grievance registry for academic, infrastructural, and administrative concerns.
- **SC/ST & OBC Welfare Cell:** Promotes equitable support, monitors scholarship disbursement (SSP/NSP), and ensures institutional inclusivity.

## 4. Sports & Athletic Development
- Guided by the Department of Physical Education, organizing annual inter-departmental athletic meets, selection trials for VTU inter-collegiate tournaments in cricket, football, basketball, badminton, table tennis, and kabaddi.
