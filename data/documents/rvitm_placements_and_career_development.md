# RVITM Placement Statistics, Career Guidance & Campus Recruitment Report
Official Data from RV Institute of Technology and Management (RVITM), Bangalore
Website: rvitm.edu.in | Campus: Chaithanya Layout, 8th Phase, JP Nagar, Bangalore – 560076
Training and Placement Cell — Operates in association with the Central Placement Ecosystem of RVCE & RSST

## 1. Official Placement Highlights & Salary Statistics
According to the official placement records published by RV Institute of Technology and Management (RVITM):
- **Highest Placement Package:** Reached up to **₹41.5 LPA** (Lakhs Per Annum) for SDE/software engineering intern-to-full-time conversions at Tekion, BitGo, and top Tier-1 product firms, with peak offers extending up to **₹45 LPA**.
- **Average Salary Package:** Overall average across disciplines ranges between **₹11.0 LPA to ₹12.27 LPA**.
  - **Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) / Information Science (ISE):** Average package ranges from **₹11.95 LPA to ₹12.44 LPA** (with top CSE offers between ₹26.35 LPA – ₹41.5 LPA).
  - **Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE):** Average package is approximately **₹8.67 LPA** (with top ECE core/software offers up to ₹19.8 LPA – ₹29.0 LPA, including Qualcomm).
  - **Overall Institute Range:** ₹8.5 LPA to ₹12.27 LPA depending on the graduating batch and recruitment cycle.
- **Placement Success Rate:** Consistently between **80% to 95%** of eligible and registered students secure campus placements and paid corporate internships.
- **Recruiting Companies:** Over **200+ companies** visit the campus annually for recruitment drives.
