## 1. Placement Highlights & Salary Packages
R V Institute of Technology and Management (RVITM) has recorded commendable placement metrics driven by industry-aligned training, centers of excellence, and pooled campus placement synergy within the premier Rashtreeya Sikshana Samithi Trust (RSST).

Key placement salary statistics:
- **Highest Placement Package:** ₹41.5 LPA (Lakhs Per Annum) to ₹42 LPA for high-performance engineering roles and product development conversions.
- **Top Product Offers:** International/Tier-1 software product offers range from ₹26.35 LPA to ₹41.5 LPA (companies such as Tekion, BitGo, Cisco, and Deutsche Bank).
- **Average Package for CSE (Computer Science):** ₹11.95 LPA – ₹12.44 LPA.
- **Average Package for ISE (Information Science):** ₹11.62 LPA – ₹12.0 LPA.
- **Overall Institute Average Package:** ₹8.5 LPA – ₹10.5 LPA across all graduating disciplines.
- **Median Package:** Approximately ₹7.5 LPA – ₹8.0 LPA.
- **Placement Success Rate:** Consistently between 82% to 92% of eligible and registered students secure campus placements and paid internships.

## 3. Placement Eligibility Criteria & Policies
Students must satisfy the institutional placement registration criteria:
1. **Academic Cutoff:** Minimum 60% or 6.75 CGPA in aggregate throughout B.E. degree with no active backlogs at the time of the recruitment process (specific top recruiters like Amazon/Cisco may mandate 70% or 7.5 CGPA).
2. **Attendance Requirement:** Consistent attendance minimum of 80% in specialized pre-placement soft skills, competitive programming, and technical interview preparation modules.
3. **Dream Offer Policy:** A student receiving an initial job offer up to ₹7 LPA remains eligible to participate in 'Dream' company drives offering ₹12 LPA or higher. Students securing offers above ₹12 LPA are deemed placed under the Super Dream category.
