## 3. Academic Structure & VTU Affiliation
- **University System:** Fully affiliated with Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi.
- **Curriculum:** VTU Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) NEP framework with continuous internal evaluations (CIE), laboratory assessments, and semester-end examinations (SEE).
- **Calendar of Events:** Published per semester on the official RVITM Academic Calendar portal (`https://rvitm.edu.in/academic-calendar/`).
