## 1. Zero Tolerance Anti-Ragging Policy
Ragging in any form is totally prohibited within RVITM campus premises, hostels, college buses, and surrounding areas. As per the Supreme Court of India guidelines, AICTE Regulations, and Karnataka Prohibition of Ragging Act:
- Ragging is a cognizable criminal offence punishable with immediate suspension, expulsion from the institute, and handover to law enforcement authorities.
- National Anti-Ragging Toll-Free Helpline: 1800-180-5522 (24x7)
- RVITM Anti-Ragging Squad Squad Head: Dr. Prashanth Kumar, Professor & Vice Principal. Contact: +91-80-6815-5610 / antiragging@rvitm.edu.in
- Local Police Station Jurisdiction: Konanakunte Police Station, Bengaluru. Helpline: 112.

## 2. Campus Dress Code & Identity Cards
- Mandatory ID Cards: Every student must prominently display their RVITM Smart ID Card around their neck at all times inside campus, laboratory complexes, and library.
- Lost ID Card: Immediately report to the Security Office and apply for a replacement ID at Counter 2 with a penalty of Rs. 250.
- Dress Code: Students are expected to wear neat, modest, and professional attire. Round-neck t-shirts with inappropriate slogans and torn denim are discouraged. Formal attire is compulsory on Mondays, during guest lectures, and placement interviews.

## 3. Library Rules & Digital Access
- Library Working Hours: Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM | Saturdays: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM | Extended hours during examination weeks up to 10:00 PM.
- Book Circulation: Undergraduate students can borrow up to 4 books for a loan period of 14 days. Renewal is permitted once if no prior reservation exists.
- Overdue Fine: Rs. 2 per book per day beyond the due date.
- Digital Library & VTU Consortium: Access to IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, and Springer journals available across campus Wi-Fi and off-campus through remote Knimbus credentials.
