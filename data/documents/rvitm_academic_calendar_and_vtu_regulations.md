## 1. Semester Schedule & Key Dates
The Odd Semester for Academic Year 2026-27 commences on September 1, 2026.
- Commencement of Classes: September 1, 2026
- Continuous Internal Evaluation 1 (CIE-1): October 12 - October 15, 2026
- Continuous Internal Evaluation 2 (CIE-2): November 23 - November 26, 2026
- Last Working Day of Odd Semester: December 24, 2026
- Practical Examination Commencement: January 4, 2027
- Semester End Theory Examination (SEE): January 18 - February 6, 2027
- Even Semester Commencement: February 22, 2027

## 2. VTU Attendance Regulations (Clause 8.1)
Students must maintain a minimum aggregate attendance of 85% in each registered theory and laboratory subject.
- VTU Attendance Requirement: 85% mandatory in all subjects.
- Medical Condonation: Condonation up to 10% (i.e., attendance between 75% and 84%) may be granted by the Principal on genuine medical grounds upon submission of a valid medical certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner within 3 working days of returning to campus.
- Attendance Below 75%: Any student with attendance below 75% in a subject will NOT be permitted to appear for the Semester End Examination (SEE) in that course and shall be declared as 'Not Eligible' (NE). The student must re-register for the course in subsequent semesters.

## 3. Internal Assessment Scheme (CIE Regulations)
- Total CIE Marks: 50 Marks per course.
- Minimum Marks Required to Qualify for SEE: Students must secure a minimum of 40% (20 marks out of 50) in CIE to be eligible for SEE.
- CIE Composition: Two written tests of 25 marks each, plus quizzes/assignments/course projects carrying 20 marks, scaled appropriately according to VTU 2022/2026 Scheme guidelines.
- Improvement Tests: One makeup test is conducted at the discretion of the Head of Department (HoD) solely for students who missed a test due to pre-approved institutional representation or serious medical emergency.

## 4. Semester End Examination (SEE) Passing Standards
- SEE Maximum Marks: 50 Marks (converted from 100).
- Minimum SEE Passing Marks: 35% (18 marks out of 50 in SEE).
- Overall Minimum Aggregate: 40% aggregate (CIE + SEE combined minimum of 40 out of 100).
