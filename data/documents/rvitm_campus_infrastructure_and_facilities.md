# RVITM Campus Infrastructure, Laboratories, Library & Student Facilities
Official Facility Records — R V Institute of Technology and Management (RVITM), Bangalore
Website: rvitm.edu.in | Campus: Chaithanya Layout, 8th Phase, JP Nagar, Bangalore – 560076

## 1. ICT Classrooms & Centers of Excellence
- **Smart Classrooms:** All lecture halls and interactive seminar rooms are equipped with high-definition digital projection, audio-visual acoustics, smart whiteboards, and high-speed Wi-Fi to support hybrid and ICT-enabled experiential teaching.
- **Auditorium & Seminar Complexes:** State-of-the-art air-conditioned auditoriums equipped with multimedia systems for guest lectures, national technical symposiums, hackathons, and industry placement talks.

## 2. Departmental Laboratories & Research Workshops
Every engineering discipline operates specialized laboratory clusters fitted with modern industrial hardware and software toolkits:
- **Computing & AI Labs:** High-throughput computer labs running Linux/Windows workstations, GPU-enabled deep learning rigs, cloud connectivity, and IDEs for programming, web development, and algorithm analysis.
- **Electronics & Embedded Systems Labs:** Digital signal processing, VLSI design suites, microcontroller and IoT workstations, PCB fabrication test benches, and microwave communication testing gear.
- **Mechanical & CAED Workshops:** Computer-Aided Engineering Drawing (CAED) labs, CNC machining setups, thermodynamics testing rigs, and rapid prototyping workshops.
- **Basic Sciences Laboratories:** Modern physics and chemistry research labs supporting VTU practical experiments and interdisciplinary material investigations.

## 3. Central Library & Information Centre
- **Hybrid & Digital Resources:** The RVITM Library functions as a cutting-edge knowledge repository housing extensive textbook collections, reference volumes, peer-reviewed international journals, conference proceedings, and national magazines.
- **Automation & RFID Security:** Fully automated using the LIBSOFT library management software integrated with Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology for self-service book issuing, returns, and digital surveillance.
- **Digital Library & Databases:** High-speed internet terminals offering access to IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, SpringerLink, and VTU Consortium e-journals.
- **Plagiarism Detection Facilities:** Integrated with certified plagiarism screening software (Turnitin and DrillBit) for academic projects, dissertations, and research publications.
- **Library Operating Hours:** Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM | Saturday: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (Extended hours until 10:00 PM during examination seasons).

## 4. Hostel Accommodation (Boys & Girls)
- **Separate Residential Facilities:** Safe, clean, and fully furnished hostel facilities provided separately for male and female students.
- **Amenities:** Ergonomic study tables, wardrobes, 24/7 power backup, solar water heating, high-speed Wi-Fi, and round-the-clock biometric and security surveillance.
- **Dining & Mess:** Hygienic on-campus dining mess offering regular, nutritious vegetarian breakfast, lunch, and dinner following strict food safety standards.
- **Hostel Warden Office:** Resident wardens and proctors available 24/7 for student guidance, medical assistance, and pastoral care.

## 5. Transportation Services (College Bus Fleet)
- **Fleet Network:** RVITM operates a fleet of secure, GPS-tracked college buses connecting the campus with major residential and suburban hubs across Bengaluru (including Majestic, Banashankari, BTM Layout, Electronic City, Jayanagar, Bannerghatta, Kengeri, and Kanakapura Road).
- **Concessions & Pass Verification:** Students can avail institutional bus passes through the Administrative Section (Counter No. 2) at the start of each academic semester.

## 6. Sports, Gymnasium & Cafeteria
- **Sports Infrastructure:** Guided by the Department of Physical Education, offering outdoor facilities for cricket, football, basketball, and volleyball, alongside indoor arenas for table tennis, badminton, and chess.
- **Gymnasium:** Modern fitness center equipped with strength and cardiovascular conditioning equipment.
- **Campus Cafeteria:** Multi-cuisine food court serving hygienic snacks, beverages, and meals for students, faculty, and campus visitors throughout working hours.
