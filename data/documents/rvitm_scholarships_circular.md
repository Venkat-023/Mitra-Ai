## 1. State Scholarship Portal (SSP Karnataka)
Eligible Karnataka domicile students enrolled in engineering programs can apply for post-matric fee concessions and maintenance allowances through the Karnataka State Scholarship Portal (ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in).
- Eligible Categories: SC / ST / Category-1 / 2A / 2B / 3A / 3B / Minorities (Muslim, Christian, Jain, Buddhist, Parsi, Sikh).
- Income Ceiling: SC/ST family annual income up to Rs. 2.50 Lakhs (full fee reimbursement) or Rs. 10 Lakhs (partial concessions); OBC Category family annual income up to Rs. 1.0 Lakh (or 2.50 Lakhs for 2A/3A/3B categories).
- Mandate: Student bank account MUST be Aadhaar-seeded via NPCI mapping.
- College SSP Verification Officer: Smt. Anitha K., Assistant Registrar (Scholarships Desk, Room 104).
- Submission Deadline: Generally December 15 of every academic year (refer to portal notifications).

## 2. National Scholarship Portal (NSP Central Schemes)
Central Sector Scheme of Scholarships for College and University Students, Pragati Scholarship for Girl Students (AICTE), and Saksham Scholarship for Differently Abled Students.
- Pragati Scholarship: Rs. 50,000 per year towards tuition fee and incidental college expenses for admitted female students through KCET/COMEDK. Family income must not exceed Rs. 8 Lakhs per annum.
- Verification: Physical submission of printed NSP application along with bonafide certificate, 10th/12th marks cards, and income certificate to the college scholarship coordinator within 7 days of online submission.

## 3. RSST & RV Merit-cum-Means Concessions
Rashtreeya Sikshana Samithi Trust provides institutional fee concessions for meritorious and financially underprivileged students of RVITM.
- Eligibility: Minimum 8.5 CGPA in preceding semesters and demonstrated financial hardship.
- How to Apply: Submit letter addressed to The Hon. Secretary, RSST, routed through The Principal, RVITM, accompanied by parental income proof and marks cards before October 30.
