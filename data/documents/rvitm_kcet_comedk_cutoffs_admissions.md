## 1. Admission Pathways & Quotas
Admissions to undergraduate 4-year Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) programs at RVITM are conducted through three official channels:
1. **KCET (Karnataka Common Entrance Test - KEA):** Approximately 45% of total seats reserved for Karnataka domicile candidates via centralized KEA counseling.
2. **COMEDK UGET:** Approximately 30% of total seats allocated for All-India candidates through COMEDK centralized counseling.
3. **Management Quota (Institutional Admissions):** Approximately 25% of seats based on 10+2 / 12th board merit and personal counseling administered under the RSST Trust guidelines.

## 2. KCET Cutoff Closing Ranks (General Merit - GM)
Based on recent KEA centralized counseling rounds (Round 1 to Extended Round 2 / Mock allocation trends):
- **Computer Science and Engineering (CSE):** Closing rank ranges typically from **7,100 to 11,700** in General Merit (GM). Category ranks (2A, 2B, 3A, 3B, SC, ST) extend up to 14,000 – 22,000.
- **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (CSE - AI & ML):** Closing rank ranges from **8,200 to 14,000** in General Merit.
- **Information Science and Engineering (ISE):** Closing rank ranges from **10,200 to 13,900** in General Merit.
- **Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE):** Closing rank ranges from **8,100 to 14,500** in General Merit.

*Note: Cutoffs vary annually depending on applicant volume, seat matrix adjustments, and exam difficulty level. Final allocations are published on the official KEA website (cetonline.karnataka.gov.in).*

## 3. COMEDK UGET Cutoff Trends (All-India Quota)
Closing ranks across COMEDK centralized counseling rounds:
- **Computer Science & Engineering (CSE):** Approximately **14,000 to 21,200** (Round 1 to Round 3).
- **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AI & ML):** Approximately **15,800 to 22,500**.
- **Information Science & Engineering (ISE):** Approximately **16,500 to 23,000**.
- **Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE):** Approximately **18,400 to 25,000**.

## 4. Academic Programs & Approved Seat Intake
RVITM is affiliated with Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi, and approved by AICTE, New Delhi:
- **B.E. Computer Science and Engineering (CSE):** 180 Seats
- **B.E. Information Science and Engineering (ISE):** 60 Seats
- **B.E. Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AIML):** 60 Seats
- **B.E. Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE):** 60 Seats
- **Total Approved Undergrad Intake:** 360 Seats per academic year.

## 5. Eligibility Criteria
- Candidates must have passed the 10+2 / 2nd PUC / 12th standard or equivalent examination with a minimum of 45% aggregate marks in Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects along with Chemistry / Biotechnology / Biology / Computer Science / Electronics.
- For SC, ST, and Category-1 / OBC candidates from Karnataka, the minimum aggregate qualifying mark in optional subjects is 40%.
- Candidates must hold a valid rank in either KCET or COMEDK UGET for merit quota consideration.
