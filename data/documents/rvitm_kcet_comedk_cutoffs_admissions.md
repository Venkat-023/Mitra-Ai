Affiliated to VTU, Belagavi | Approved by AICTE, New Delhi | Managed by RSST Trust

## 1. Admission Pathways & Quotas
Admissions to undergraduate 4-year B.E. programs at RVITM are conducted through three official channels:
1. **KCET (Karnataka Common Entrance Test - KEA):** For Karnataka domicile candidates through KEA centralized counseling.
2. **COMEDK UGET:** For All-India candidates and Karnataka candidates via COMEDK centralized counseling.
3. **Management Quota (Institutional Admissions):** Based on 10+2 / 12th board merit under the Rashtreeya Sikshana Samithi Trust (RSST) guidelines.

## 2. KCET Cutoff Closing Ranks (General Merit - GM)
Based on official KEA counseling trends across rounds:
- **Computer Science & Engineering (CSE):** Closing rank ranges typically from **7,200 to 11,700** in General Merit (GM).
- **CSE (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning - AIML):** Closing rank ranges from **8,200 to 13,300** in General Merit.
- **Information Science & Engineering (ISE):** Closing rank ranges from **10,200 to 13,900** in General Merit.
- **Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE):** Closing rank ranges from **12,000 to 15,800** in General Merit.
- *Reserved category closing ranks (2A, 2B, 3A, 3B, SC, ST) extend up to 14,000 – 22,000 depending on category allocation.*

## 3. COMEDK UGET Cutoff Trends (All-India / Unreserved Quota)
Based on COMEDK centralized counseling closing rounds:
- **Computer Science & Engineering (CSE):** Approximately **7,800 to 10,000** in Round 1, closing around **14,000 to 21,200** in extended rounds.
- **CSE (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning):** Approximately **10,000 to 14,000** in Round 1, closing around **15,800 to 22,500**.
- **Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE):** Approximately **12,300 to 15,500** in Round 1, closing around **18,400 to 25,000**.
- **Information Science & Engineering (ISE):** Approximately **12,000 to 16,500** (Round 1), closing around **16,500 to 23,000**.

*Note: Cutoffs fluctuate each academic year based on test-taker volume, seat matrix updates, and counseling rounds. Real-time lists are published on cetonline.karnataka.gov.in and comedk.org.*

## 4. Academic Programs & Approved Seat Matrix
RVITM offers four undergraduate engineering disciplines and postgraduate studies:
- **B.E. Computer Science & Engineering (CSE):** 180 Seats
- **B.E. Information Science & Engineering (ISE):** 60 Seats
- **B.E. Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AIML):** 60 Seats
- **B.E. Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE):** 60 Seats
- **Master of Computer Applications (MCA):** Approved post-graduate program.
- **Total Approved Undergrad Intake:** 360 Seats per year.

## 5. Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum 45% aggregate marks in 10+2 / 2nd PUC / 12th standard or equivalent in Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects along with Chemistry / Biotechnology / Biology / Computer Science / Electronics.
- 40% aggregate in optional subjects for SC, ST, and Category-1 / OBC candidates from Karnataka.
- A valid rank in KCET or COMEDK UGET is mandatory for merit seat allocation.
