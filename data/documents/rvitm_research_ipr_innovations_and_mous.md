# RVITM Research Initiatives, Intellectual Property (IPR), Innovations & Industry Collaborations

## 1. Research & Academic Innovation Ecosystem
RV Institute of Technology and Management (RVITM) fosters an integrated environment combining academic rigor, scientific inquiry, patentable engineering research, and practical industrial implementation across undergraduate (B.E.) and postgraduate (MCA) disciplines.
- **Affiliation & Research Alignment:** Affiliated with Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi, and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi.
- **Interdisciplinary Research Clusters:** Faculty and student project teams actively conduct applied research in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Edge Computing, Embedded IoT Systems, VLSI Design, Robotics, and Sustainable Mechanical Systems.
- **Library Research Resources:** Centralized digital database subscriptions, IEEE Xplore access, science journals, and e-learning platforms provided through VTU Consortium and national digital libraries.

## 2. InventX Platform & Student Innovation
- **InventX Platform:** RVITM’s flagship collaborative innovation platform designed to bridge the gap between technical concepts and production implementations.
- **Key Objectives of InventX:**
  • Connects student innovators, faculty researchers, alumni founders, industry experts, and venture mentors.
  • Facilitates interdisciplinary prototyping, intellectual property evaluation, and early-stage startup incubation.
  • Provides technical mentorship on scalable software engineering, patent drafting, and seed funding pathways.
- **Hackathons & Competitions:**
  • Regular host of national-level hackathons including **FantomCode**, an open-innovation competition where multi-departmental teams solve corporate and social challenges.
  • Student participation in Smart India Hackathon (SIH), VTU technical fests, IEEE student branch design competitions, and open-source developer events.

## 3. Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) & Patent Portfolio
RVITM maintains an active Institutional Intellectual Property (IPR) Cell that encourages students and faculty to file and publish patents for novel algorithms, hardware designs, and engineering solutions.
- **AIKYAM National IPR Conclave:** RVITM hosts AIKYAM, a premier national conference and conclave on Intellectual Property Rights, bringing together patent attorneys, academics, and industry experts to evaluate student patent submissions.
- **Patents Published & Filed:**
  • Utility and design patents in AI/ML-driven frameworks, deep learning diagnostic models, and Vision Transformer (ViT) architectures.
  • IoT-enabled smart environmental sensing devices, autonomous robotic navigation, and automotive electronic monitoring systems.
  • Patent application assistance provided to students with institutional documentation and fee grants for verified innovations.

## 4. Industry MoUs, Corporate Collaborations & Skill Labs
RVITM maintains strategic Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs) with leading tech enterprises and research laboratories to bridge academia and industry:
- **Corporate Partners:**
  • **Sensesemi Technologies:** Research and training on high-performance semiconductor design, ARM-based SoCs, and edge AI execution.
  • **Forus Industry Collaborations:** Joint development in industrial automation, intelligent instrumentation, and vision-guided systems.
  • **Tech Skill Labs:** Specialized practical labs focusing on PyTorch, ExecuTorch, TinyML, Linux kernel programming, and cloud-native Kubernetes deployment.
- **Industry Internships & Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs):** Centralized corporate access through the RV Educational Institutions (RVEI) network, enabling students to convert academic internships into full-time roles starting from their 6th and 7th semesters.
