## 1. Purpose of Bonafide Certificate
A Bonafide Certificate confirms a student's active enrollment, branch, semester, and USN (University Seat Number) at RVITM. It is typically required for:
- State and National Scholarship applications (SSP, NSP).
- Education loan processing at nationalized and private banks.
- Passport applications, visa processing, and police verification.
- BMTC / KSRTC student bus pass concessional verification.
- Internship verifications and technical conference registrations.

## 2. Step-by-Step Application Procedure
To apply for an official Bonafide Certificate:
1. Download the Bonafide Application Form from the college intranet portal (ERP student login) or collect a physical form from the Academic Section counter (Counter No. 2, Administrative Block, Ground Floor).
2. Fill in student details: Full Name (as per 10th standard marks card), USN, Department, Current Semester, and specific purpose of certificate.
3. Attach required enclosures: Photocopy of student college ID card, current semester fee paid receipt, and proof of purpose (e.g. bank loan letter or passport application form).
4. Obtain sign-off/recommendation from your Department Proctor (Mentor) and Head of Department (HoD).
5. Submit the verified application to the Academic Section / Registrar Office Counter No. 2 between 10:00 AM and 1:00 PM on working days.

## 3. Processing Timeline & Fees
- Standard Turnaround Time: 2 to 3 working days from the date of submission.
- Application Fee: Nil for regular academic purposes and first 2 copies per academic year. Duplicate copies or urgent same-day processing fees may incur a nominal processing fee of Rs. 100 paid at the accounts counter.
- Collection: Collect the signed and sealed certificate in person from Counter 2 between 2:30 PM and 4:30 PM by presenting the original college ID card.
